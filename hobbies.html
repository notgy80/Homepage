My Hobbies

I've always liked to think that I have 3 main strengths: Academics, Gaming, and Playing Sports. During my free time, I can always be found either playing games, such as Clash of Clans, Clash Royale, BTD Battles, and more recently, Brawl Stars, PES and Minecraft, or sports such as Badminton or Football. Of course, I also enjoy spending time with my good friends, whether it is while doing one of my hobbies or just going out to have a meal. I also enjoy travelling overseas a lot to visit world-famous attractions and try out various cuisines.
