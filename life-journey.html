My Life Journey

For my whole life, I have lived in Singapore. When I was 7, I went to West Grove Primary School for 6 years. After, I went to NUS High School of Mathematics and Science, where I spent the next 6 years under the Intergrated Programme (IP). I graduated with the NUS High School Diploma, an A Level equivalent certification, majoring in Math, Physics, Chemistry and Economics. Currently, I am serving the 2 years of my National Service as a Security Trooper in the Singapore Armed Forces (SAF). In 2027, I will embark on my undergraduate studies in the University of Cambridge under the Defence Science and Technology Agency (DSTA) Scholarship.
