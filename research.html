About My Research

When I was a y4 student in NUS High School, I was encouraged to take up a research project. That was when my research journey of 3 years began, where I worked in the Institute of Materials Research and Engineering (IMRE), Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A*STAR). Under the mentorship of Asst. Prof. Ady Suwardi and Dr Tan Xian Yi, I researched on thermoelectric materials, which are materials that can convert heat into electricity. My research has led me to publish 2 papers in peer reviewed journals, and also participate in numerous research competitions, the most notable being the International Science and Engineering Fair (ISEF).

My Research Journey

A*STAR Research Internship 1st year Under Asst. Prof. Ady Suwardi
▾
Worked on the thermoelectric material AgSbTe2, used doping to increase its waste heat harvesting efficiency. Published a paper: Non-equilibrium strategy for enhancing thermoelectric properties and improving stability of AgSbTe2 in the peer reviewed journal Nano Energy.
Singapore Science and Engineering Fair Awarded Gold, 1 special award and selected to represent Singapore at ISEF
▾
The Singapore Science and Engineering Fair (SSEF) is a annual competition where students can present the research that they conducted in the past year. Their research and presentation will be graded by judges who are distinguished researchers in Singapore. I presented my research done in A*STAR on AgSbTe2. SSEF 2023 Results
National STEM Talent Search Awarded 2nd place among the best student researchers in Singapore
▾
The National STEM Talent Search (NSTS) is an annual invitational competition where gold, silver or bronze awardees in the Singapore Science and Engineering Fair can compete in. The judges look to award promising student researchers who has the potential to contribute to Singapore's research scene. In the final round, I had the opportunity to present my research to Nobel Laureate Prof. Konstantin Novoselov, and was awarded second place in the competition.
NSTS 2023 Results
International Science and Engineering Fair 4th award in the Energy: Sustainable Materials and Design Category
▾
The International Science and Engineering Fair (ISEF) is the world's largest and most prestigious global pre-college STEM competition. I was one of 6 projects to represent Singapore, and presented my research to distinguished researchers from around the world. This experience allowed me to gain valuable insight on what research other students are conducting, and I also learnt a lot of soft skills such as communication and time-management skills. ISEF 2023 Results
ST Yau High School Science Award (Asia) Silver Prize in the Chemistry Category
▾
The ST Yau High School Science Award (Asia) is a regional competition where students selected get the opportunity to fly to Hong Kong to present their research to judges. This competition required very deep knowledge from me in my research, as I had to do a detailed 15 minute presentation followed by a gruelling 10 minute defence of my research.
